# Volume 2D -- Train Operations

## Player Controls

Each line exposes: - Frequency - Local - Balanced - Express

The simulation generates train movements automatically.

## Express Services

Express trains stop only at major destinations by default. Advanced mode
allows manual stop editing.

## Capacity

Additional frequency reduces waiting time but increases costs.

## Congestion

Busy routes can become saturated. Players may build: - Passing loops -
Additional tracks - High-speed bypasses

Watching express trains overtake stopping services should become one of
the game's signature visual moments.
