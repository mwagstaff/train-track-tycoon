# Volume 2C -- Economy & Revenue

## Revenue

Revenue comes from: - Passenger fares - Distance travelled - Premium
high-speed services - Future freight

## Costs

Capital: - Track - Bridges - Tunnels - Stations - Trains

Operating: - Maintenance - Energy - Staff - Infrastructure upkeep

## Career Mode

Profit funds expansion. Loans are available but incur interest.

## Zen Mode

Budget is unlimited but all figures remain visible: - Revenue - Costs -
Profit - Network value - Commercial viability

The economy should feel believable without becoming an accounting
exercise.
