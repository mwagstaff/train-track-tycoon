# Volume 2B -- Accessibility & Happiness

## Objective

Happiness is the primary score and represents how useful the railway is.

## Score Components

  Factor                     Weight
  ------------------------ --------
  Reachable destinations        30%
  Destination importance        20%
  Journey time                  20%
  Frequency                     15%
  Reliability                   10%
  Crowding                       5%

## Diminishing Returns

Extra frequency provides progressively smaller benefits.

## Local Scores

Every settlement has its own happiness score.

Example feedback: - Excellent London access - Poor regional
connectivity - Overcrowded peak trains

## Global Score

The network score is the weighted average of all settlements.

Players should always understand *why* the score changed.
