# Volume 2E -- Population Growth & Station Evolution

## Population

Improved accessibility increases: - Population - Employment - Tourism

Growing settlements generate additional passenger demand.

## Station Evolution

Stations automatically upgrade:

1.  Halt
2.  Local Station
3.  Town Station
4.  Major Station
5.  Interchange
6.  Terminus

Higher tiers unlock: - More platforms - Greater capacity - Improved
visual appearance

## Visual Feedback

Stations become larger and more detailed over time.

The railway should visibly evolve from a handful of rural halts into a
dense national network, rewarding long-term play.
