## Design Context

### Users

TrainTrack Tycoon is for iPhone players who enjoy railway networks, transport strategy, and watching a system they built come alive. The primary context is portrait, one-handed touch play: choose real British stations, build a useful service quickly, inspect its performance, and then enjoy watching trains operate over a real map.

### Brand Personality

Calm, premium, and alive. The interface should feel immediately understandable and trustworthy like a polished native iOS app, while preserving the charm and clarity of a modern transport game. Feedback is concise and encouraging rather than noisy or arcade-like.

### Aesthetic Direction

The map and railway are the visual heroes. Use natural map colours, bright colour-coded railway lines, restrained native materials, subtle shadows, rounded geometry, and purposeful motion. Apple Maps supplies the interaction reference, Mini Metro supplies visual clarity, and Transport Tycoon supplies the feeling of a living world. Avoid clutter, decorative dashboards, excessive cards, neon effects, and UI that obscures or prevents direct map manipulation. Support portrait iPhone first, Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, colour-independent meaning, and Reduce Motion.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve the map: floating UI occupies only the space its visible content needs and never blocks gestures through visually empty regions.
2. Make the next action obvious: building, inspecting, changing frequency, and understanding results should require no timetable knowledge or tutorial.
3. Reveal detail progressively: show a compact network pulse by default and richer station or train information only after selection.
4. Design for touch and one hand: use native interaction patterns and at least 44-point targets while keeping map pan, pinch, and double-tap fluid.
5. Let change be visible but calm: trains, construction, station growth, and notifications communicate a living railway with short, purposeful, reduced-motion-aware animation.
