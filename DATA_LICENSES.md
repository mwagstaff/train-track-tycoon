# Data licences

## OpenStreetMap railway data

Railway geometry and topology are derived from OpenStreetMap data and distributed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors

- Copyright and licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The bundled routing graph records its source snapshot, build metadata, checksums, filtering rules, and attribution in its `metadata` object. The current source snapshot is dated 14 August 2026 and was obtained from the Geofabrik Great Britain extract by the TrainTrack UK offline routing toolchain.

## Station catalogue

The POC reuses the local TrainTrack UK station catalogue with the explicit authorization of this project's owner, who is also the author and owner of TrainTrack UK and its station data. The catalogue is cleared for use in and distribution with TrainTrack Tycoon.

## Apple MapKit

The basemap is rendered by Apple MapKit. Apple-provided legal notices must remain visible and must not be obscured by game interface elements.
